# Digest: fantasy-top (round 1)

**Summary:** Fantasy Top is dead: it announced shutdown on 21 May 2026, sunset its prediction-market and jackpot modes immediately, ran final competitions to 18 June 2026, and closed the site 25 June 2026 — with the team's own post-mortem being "we tried to put crypto on top of a model that was never built for crypto." The arc is a textbook reflexive spike: launched on Blast 1 May 2024, hit ~$9.31M of ETH fees in a single week (5th most profitable crypto protocol behind only BTC, ETH, Lido, Uniswap), paid $1.25M to hero influencers inside week one, raised $4.25M from Dragonfly in Dec 2024 — then lost 93% of monthly fees (to ~$200k) and 80% of active users (to ~2,000) by mid-2025, migrated Blast→Base, pivoted to prediction markets, drew "soft rugpull" allegations from angels in March 2026, and folded. The loop was: buy demand-priced packs (~0.4 ETH at peak) → field a 5-card deck of crypto-Twitter "heroes" → score = hero Twitter engagement × card rarity multiplier (1.5x/2x/2.5x) → league leaderboards paying ETH, packs and Blast Gold; heroes earned 1.5% of their own card's trading volume plus a share of 10% of pack revenue. It worked because it paid its own distribution channel in the asset and settled a status game CT already plays; it failed because revenue was collectible trading volume rather than recurring play (non-recurring and reflexive), the social-engagement oracle was bot-gameable enough to force an early cancellation of the first competition, and 0.4 ETH pack pricing made capital rather than prediction skill the dominant term. On the secondary sweep: Football.Fun (Base, tokenized athlete exposure, FUN token) is the live analog, Maincard.io (TON) and Sorare persist, DraftKings killed Reignmakers amid a class action — but nothing retrieved this run drafts on-chain events as the players, and nothing uses proof-based settlement; that combination appears genuinely unoccupied.

## Contradictions
- The Block (11 Mar 2026) reports the founder claiming 'multiple years of runway' and that the company was fully self-funded on product revenue; ten weeks later (21 May 2026) Decrypt reports the shutdown with the team saying they 'didn't have the conviction to keep going.' Not strictly contradictory (conviction vs solvency), but the runway claim and the shutdown timing sit uneasily together and validate investor complaints about communication.
- The team stated in November 2025 that the core game feature was not shutting down (per The Block); it shut down in May-June 2026.
- DL News fetch rendered the trough month as 'June 2024' while the article was published 15 July 2025 and frames the decline as since the May 2024 debut — the figure is almost certainly June 2025. Flagged as medium confidence rather than asserted.
