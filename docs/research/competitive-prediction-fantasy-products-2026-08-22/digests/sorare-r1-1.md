# Digest: sorare (round 1)

**Summary:** Sorare's loop is a weekly 5-player fantasy lineup (GK/DEF/MID/FWD/flex, captain +50%) built from Pro cards in four print-capped scarcity tiers (Limited 1,000 / Rare 100 / Super Rare 10 / Unique 1) plus free non-collectible Common cards, layered with XP that accrues from submitting training lineups even when a card has no fixture, 1-5% same-club collection bonuses, tiered divisions (Champion/Challenger/Contender), and a free head-to-head mode (Rivals) where entries are priced by L15 form under a budget cap. The economy collapsed hard: sales volume fell from $343m (2022) to a $2.7m monthly low in June 2025, revenue from ~€143m (2022) to €43m (2024), cash from $230m to a projected $41m by end-2025 — driven by supply dilution from issuing cards through both auctions and rewards, by removing the ETH threshold payout in Sept 2024 (which cratered engagement for ~11 months), and by fixed exclusive-licensing costs against collapsing volume. The August 2025 reintroduction of Hot Streaks (cumulative payouts for consecutive high scores) drove volume back over $20m and an all-time-high >1m monthly transactions, and Sorare has shifted In-Season rewards toward cash prizes. Trajectory over the last 12 months is retrenchment: Solana migration announced Oct 2025 (cards bridged retaining rarity/numbering/season/XP, ~$10m infra saving, token "explored"), then a 35% layoff in Nov 2025 with CTO Adrien Montfort stepping back, US ops nearly shut, ~€500m of league/club commitments renegotiated, and a stated profitability target of end-2026. The decisive design signal is that cardholders grew 33% to 378,000 in 2024 while revenue fell 27% — the manager fantasy retained people after speculation died; the monetization design is what broke. Copy the participation-earned XP, multiplexed card utility across many competition surfaces, the L15 budget-capped draft, and collection bonuses; avoid paying rewards in the same asset you sell, issuance with no sink, loot-box randomness (Essence), and tier sprawl.

## Contradictions
- 2024 loss magnitude: Sporting Crypto reports -$117m EBITDA for 2024, while The Crypto Times (citing The Big Whale) reports losses exceeding €220m in 2024. Plausibly EBITDA vs net loss including licence-commitment impairments, but unverified this run.
- Narrative framing: ReadWrite headline says Sorare is 'on the verge of collapse' while Sporting Crypto documents an August 2025 volume recovery to $20m+ and an all-time-high transaction count. Both can be true — volume recovered off a very low base while cash runway continued to shrink toward a projected $41m.
- Revenue currency/unit mismatch: Sporting Crypto quotes 2022-2024 revenue in USD ($167m/$69m/$50m) while French-sourced reporting quotes EUR (€143m/€59m/€43m). Same underlying figures at different FX, not independent confirmation.
