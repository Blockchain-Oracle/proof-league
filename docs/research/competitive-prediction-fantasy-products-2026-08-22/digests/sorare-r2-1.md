# Digest: sorare (round 2)

**Summary:** The round-1 premise on Rivals is stale: Sorare Football is now organised as three modes — Arcade (ex-Sorare Set), Pro (Hot Streaks vs fixed targets), and Arena (all PvP leaderboards) — and Rivals no longer exists, with its removal being the loudest complaint in first-person reviews. Defeating the help-centre browser wall via firecrawl yielded exact first-party mechanics that round 1 could not get: Hot Streaks (4 lives / 4 lineup tries per step, anytime entry, one-matchday step clock), Arena (Tue/Fri 4PM CET, Divisions abolished, auto-triggering DNP sub slots that void Multi-Club/Cap bonuses), and the full Essence economy (1,000 Essence → draw of 10, keep 1; published tier criteria and per-draw odds tables; Clue costs of 1,000–2,000). Two gaps closed by mechanism: the Essence sink is a degressive cross-season conversion (larger balances reduced more) plus hard per-scarcity caps, and the card sink is Sealing — a soft, reversible lockup making cards untradeable/unplayable with unsealing throttled to one card per scarcity per two weeks, feeding Vault Levels 1–20 worth 1–20% reward boosts that apply only to In-Season/Classic leaderboards, not Hot Streaks, Missions or Arena. The primary Solana post (Oct 8 2025) confirms cNFT minting preserved scarcity, serial, season and XP, that ETH balances went to Base rather than Solana, that fees were unchanged, and that the token language was explicitly hedged ("possibly expanding our own ecosystem powered by a Sorare token") — weaker than secondary coverage implies. Apple's official review RSS supplied dated verbatim user voice (Rivals removal, MLB commons removal driving pay-to-win complaints, compounding UI complexity, seasonal card obsolescence), though the 4.76/3,603 aggregate is inflated by an aggressive review prompt — a 5-star review is titled "They ruined the game this year". Trustpilot (403 even via stealth proxy), Reddit (firecrawl policy-blocked and JSON-blocked) and the Big Whale restructuring figures remain unretrieved, so the −$117m vs €220m contradiction is still unresolved.

## Contradictions
- Round-1 brief assumed Rivals is a current mode and asked for 'the exact Rivals tactic list'. First-party help documentation (current as of ~June 2026) lists only Arcade, Pro and Arena, and App Store reviews from April-July 2025 describe Rivals being removed. The round-1 premise is stale; Rivals was discontinued.
- The -$117m vs EUR 220m Sorare loss contradiction from round 1 remains UNRESOLVED. The Big Whale primary article was not retrievable (only 2022-era Sorare coverage plus a paywalled premium piece surfaced via domain-restricted search). No new evidence either way was obtained this run.
- Apple's aggregate rating (4.761 across 3,603 ratings) contradicts the tenor of the actual recent review text, which is heavily negative. The feed itself explains the gap: an aggressive review prompt produces 5-star ratings attached to hostile review bodies. Aggregate app-store ratings for Sorare should not be cited as a satisfaction signal.
- Secondary coverage of a 'Sorare token' appears firmer than the primary source warrants. Sorare's own wording is 'possibly expanding our own ecosystem powered by a Sorare token', with 'possibly' emphasised in the original - exploratory, not announced.
- Round 1 recorded 'no card sink found'. That is now partially contradicted: Sealing is a genuine supply-lock (cards become unsellable, untradeable, unplayable, with unsealing throttled to one per scarcity per two weeks), and Essence has a degressive cross-season conversion plus hard caps. Neither is a burn, so 'no destruction mechanism' still holds.
