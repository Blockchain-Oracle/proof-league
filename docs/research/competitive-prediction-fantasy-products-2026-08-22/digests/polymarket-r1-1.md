# Digest: polymarket (round 1)

**Summary:** Polymarket is a central-limit-order-book exchange (explicitly not an AMM) where outcome shares trade $0.00–$1.00 and the docs state outright that "the price directly represents the market's belief in the probability of that outcome"; complementary Yes+No orders mint $1.00 of ERC1155 outcome tokens, winners redeem at $1.00 via the CTF collateral adapter, and collateral appears to be mid-migration from USDC to a house token, pUSD. Monetization is taker-only: fee = C × feeRate × p × (1−p), peaking at p=0.50, with per-category feeRates from 0.07 (Crypto, ≈1.75% at the peak) down to 0 (Geopolitics), and makers paid daily rebates out of taker fees. Settlement is the structural weak point: a ~$750 bond proposal, a 2-hour challenge window, a second proposal on first dispute, then escalation to a UMA DVM token-holder vote — 4–6 days worst case, and "once finalized by UMA, outcomes are immutable" with Polymarket disclaiming any ability to correct them. Documented failures are specific and recent: the ~$60M "Strategy sells any Bitcoin by May 31, 2026?" market escalated to a UMA vote over whether the rule required May disclosure or only May on-chain execution (The Defiant, ~June 1–2 2026); a ~$7M Ukraine–Russia market resolved "Yes" with no deal, one holder casting ~25% of the vote via ~5M UMA across three wallets; and a WSJ investigation found >60% of active UMA voters linkable to live Polymarket accounts with ~1 in 5 disputes involving financially interested voters. Business trajectory is strong and institutional — ICE has put ~$1.64B in across Oct 2025 and March 2026, talks reported at $15B and >$20B, $10.6B notional volume in March 2026 (~6x six months prior), CFTC DCM status since Nov 2025 and a US relaunch Dec 2 2025 — yet as of April 2026 Polymarket US was still in beta with little trading while the firm lobbied to reopen the offshore book to Americans. The wedge is unambiguous: Trustpilot sits at 1.5/5, and the dominant Reddit complaint is not payments but grading — an anonymous, conflicted, bond-gated token vote deciding what is true, plus withdrawal delays, locked funds, and a mobile app that reportedly cannot connect a wallet to withdraw.
