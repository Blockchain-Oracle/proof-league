# Digest: fantasy-top (round 2)

**Summary:** Closed the fee-curve lead with primary data: DeFiLlama's API (the web UI was blocked; the correct slug is 'fantasy.top', not 'fantasy-top') gives fantasy.top $7,053,188 all-time fees on Blast, with monthly fees peaking at ~$717k in Sep 2024 and decaying ~85% to ~$106k by Jul 2025 in an oscillating downtrend where each local peak is lower than the last — the signature of an economy funded by new deposits. Critically, the series reads zero from Aug 2025 onward despite the platform operating until mid-2026, so the adapter broke; post-Jul-2025 zeros are a tracking gap, not evidence. The founder post-mortem is only reachable via secondary quotation (x.com unfetchable) and is attributed to co-founder 'Kipit' — correcting round 1's 'Travis Bickle' — with the core diagnosis that TCGs succeed when they are games first and assets second, whereas 'the card is financial before it is anything else' in crypto, plus 'a token before product-market fit is poison'. The V2 prediction-market and jackpot modes remain undocumented anywhere retrievable (docs.fantasy.top does not resolve) and stay an open gap. Football.Fun has rebranded to Sport.fun (football.fun 302-redirects to sport.fun/football): athlete shares are non-expiring spot-market positions priced by trading flow rather than by performance algorithm, contests auto-enter any 100+ share squad on a near-continuous Fri-Mon/Tue-Thu cadence, scoring settles against Opta Analyst's commercial feed, rewards pay in points redeemable for more shares rather than stablecoins, and revenue is the trade fee — with $FUN (1B fixed supply, Base, listed 2026-01-15) taking 40% of marketplace fee revenue in buybacks, meaning the game is the funnel, the trading fee is the revenue, and the token is the wrapper. On the on-chain-entities gap, one live product surfaced — CoinDraft on Solana, drafting 7 tokens under a 50-point salary cap and paying winners on-chain — but it scores on token % price gain, so it still settles against a price feed; nothing was found that drafts wallets/protocols/validators scored on chain state, and nothing anywhere uses proof-based settlement.

## Contradictions
- Trade fee for Football.Fun/Sport.fun: Blocmates states a 5% base trade fee on all buys/sells; Bitrue states a 3% marketplace transaction fee. Most likely a fee change across the Football.Fun -> Sport.fun rebrand (the 3% figure is from the newer token-launch-era source), but unverified. Do not cite a single fee number without re-checking the primary docs.
- Founder attribution: round 1 recorded the Fantasy Top shutdown post as coming from founder 'Travis Bickle'; this run's retrieval attributes the statement to co-founder 'Kipit'. Either two co-founders exist or one attribution is wrong. Unresolved without the original X thread.
- DeFiLlama shows zero fees from 2025-08 onward while the platform demonstrably operated (including a final season with a $100,000 jackpot) until mid-2026. The data and the timeline contradict; the resolution is almost certainly a broken/abandoned Blast adapter rather than zero activity.
