## Candidates
### 1. Nouns daily auction — the September 2026 run (#2008 → #2025) — fit 5/5
- **question_example**: What will today's Noun sell for — over or under X ETH?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Nouns Auction House proxy 0x830BD73E4184ceF73443C15111a1DF14e495C706. Anyone calls settleCurrentAndCreateNewAuction(); that ONE tx emits AuctionSettled(uint256 indexed nounId, address winner, uint256 amount), topic0 = 0xc9f72b276a388619c6d185d146697036241880c36654b1a3ffdad07c24038d99 (computed this run with cast keccak). nounId = topic1; winner = data word 0; **amount (data word 1) = winning bid in wei = the answer**. Same receipt also emits AuctionCreated for the next Noun, so one tx closes today and opens tomorrow.
- **cadence**: daily — one auction ends every ~24h at ~02:29 UTC (settlement tx timing drifts slightly since anyone can poke it)
- **uncertainty**: Live English auction with a 5-minute anti-snipe extension. The clearing price depends entirely on who shows up and how hard they fight in the last minutes — not computable or simulatable in advance.
- **manipulation**: SEVERE ENDOGENEITY — the cheapest attack is to simply be the bidder. Whoever bets 'over 30 ETH' can bid 31 ETH and win both sides; cost is the marginal bid, small in absolute terms, and they keep the NFT. Mitigations: wide over/under bands, position caps relative to historical clearing prices, or move the question to something one actor doesn't control (number of distinct bidders, whether the auction entered extension, last-two-digits of the wei amount). NOTE: recent clearing prices were NOT verified this run (archive reads blocked) — measure before setting bands.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — the settleCurrentAndCreateNewAuction() tx receipt containing AuctionSettled proves nounId, winner and winning amount outright.
- **live_evidence**: Read live this run: eth_call auction() on 0x830BD73E4184ceF73443C15111a1DF14e495C706 at block 25,813,753 (2026-08-22 22:45 UTC) returned nounId=1999, amount=0, startTime=2026-08-22 02:29 UTC, endTime=2026-08-23 02:29 UTC, settled=false — an auction is running at this moment. NounsToken.totalSupply() = 1974. Source: https://ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com (Ethereum mainnet)
- **notes**: The spine of the demo week. NounsToken.mint() hard-codes `if (_currentNounId <= 1820 && _currentNounId % 10 == 0)`, so the every-10th-Noun Nounders reward ENDED at Noun 1820 — ids now increment exactly 1/day and the schedule is arithmetic, not estimate. ### 3. The 2,550,000th beacon-chain deposit — fit 4/5
- **question_example**: Which day does Ethereum record its 2,550,000th staking deposit?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Beacon deposit contract 0x00000000219ab540356cBB839Cbe05303d7705Fa emits DepositEvent(bytes pubkey, bytes withdrawal_credentials, bytes amount, bytes signature, bytes index) on every deposit. **The `index` field carries the running deposit counter inside the event itself**, so the tx whose decoded index == 2549999 (0-indexed) IS the proof. `index` is an ABI-encoded bytes holding a little-endian uint64 — decode accordingly.
- **cadence**: continuous (deposits arrive many times daily); round-number milestones recur every 50k/100k deposits forever
- **uncertainty**: Depends on validator entry demand — how many people choose to stake in the coming weeks. Future human/market behavior, not computable in advance.
- **manipulation**: Expensive to force meaningfully: 6,765 deposits × 32 ETH ≈ 216k ETH of capital to pull the counter forward. BUT a single motivated actor CAN snipe the exact index if the question is 'who wins slot #2,550,000' — so keep the question on WHICH DAY it lands, not who lands it.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — and it is the cleanest in this whole slice: no keeper, no snapshot, no aggregation. One receipt, one event field.
- **live_evidence**: Read live this run: eth_call get_deposit_count() on 0x00000000219ab540356cBB839Cbe05303d7705Fa returned 0x83ce260000000000 = little-endian uint64 = 2,543,235 deposits at block 25,813,753 (2026-08-22 22:45 UTC) — 6,765 short of 2,550,000. Source: https://ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com
- **notes**: OPEN GAP: I could not measure the deposit rate — the public RPC rejects archive reads ('Archive requests require a personal token'), so I will not date the crossing. Fix by polling get_deposit_count() twice 24h apart before committing to a date. Worth including in the demo specifically because it shows a DIFFERENT settlement shape from Nouns: a running counter carried in an event field.

### 5. Glamsterdam hard-fork activation, proven by a new-opcode probe tx (Sepolia in window; mainnet later) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: Will Sepolia be running Glamsterdam by date D?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Glamsterdam ships NEW EVM OPCODES — SLOTNUM (EIP-7843) and backward-compatible SWAPN/DUPN/EXCHANGE (EIP-8024). Deploy a probe contract whose function executes SLOTNUM and emits ForkLive(uint256 slot). Pre-fork that tx REVERTS (receipt status = 0); post-fork it SUCCEEDS (status = 1) and emits. One receipt, decodable status, decodable event — the fork becomes a POSITIVE settlement transaction instead of an absence proof. The first successful poke is the answer.
- **cadence**: one-off per network (Sepolia, then mainnet); recurs roughly twice a year under Ethereum's new biannual fork cadence
- **uncertainty**: Client-team readiness and devnet/testnet stability decide the date, and it has already slipped repeatedly (originally June 2026). Genuinely unknowable in advance — no date exists yet to simulate against.
- **manipulation**: Essentially unmanipulable by a bettor — fork timing is set by core devs and client releases, not by anyone who can take a position. The residual risk is INFORMATION asymmetry: core devs and client teams know the date before the market does. That is an insider-edge problem, not a manipulation problem.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT (probe variant) — the fork itself is block-level state with no receipt; the probe tx manufactures the receipt. Must be framed positively with a settlement poke AT the deadline, never as 'the fork did not happen'.
- **live_evidence**: Ethereum Foundation announced the Platåberget testnet on 2026-08-17: 'The Glamsterdam fork on the testnet is scheduled for 20th August.' Sepolia and Hoodi fork after devnets stabilise; mainnet has no announced date. EIP-7773 (Glamsterdam meta) still shows activation rows unfilled: 'rows in the table above will be filled as activation times are decided by client teams.' Sources: https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/08/17/plataberget-testnet and https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7773
- **notes**: Highest narrative value in the slice, real date risk. Mainnet is realistically AFTER the window (guidance is ~30 days from the ACD call following a successful Hoodi fork); Sepolia inside the window is plausible and Sepolia is explicitly allowed by the referee. Secondary reporting is contradictory (end-Aug vs Q3 vs Q4 2026), which is itself the tell that no date exists. Worth building even if the date slips — the probe contract is the point, because it demonstrates converting a non-tx event into a positive single-tx proof.

### 7. On-chain DAO proposal resolution (opportunistic) — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: How many FOR votes does proposal #N end with?
- **settlement_mechanism**: For a PASS: ProposalQueued / ProposalExecuted from the relevant Governor is a clean single-tx proof. For the tally (the better question): SNAPSHOT-KEEPER reading governor.proposalVotes(id) after the voting deadline and emitting the three numbers in one event.
- **cadence**: irregular / ad hoc — DAO calendars are not scheduled far enough ahead to plan a demo around
- **uncertainty**: Depends on delegate turnout and whip behavior — genuinely uncertain, especially the tally.
- **manipulation**: Requires acquiring or borrowing governance tokens to move a tally — expensive for large DAOs, but flash-loan/delegation attacks are a live concern for smaller ones. Delegates who vote can also bet: endogeneity again.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES for PASS only — and that asymmetry is the problem: a proposal that FAILS emits nothing, so 'will it pass?' is half an absence proof. The tally variant (ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT) is positive in both directions and more interesting to bet on.
- **live_evidence**: unverified — no on-chain governance proposal was confirmed live or scheduled into Sep 1–18, 2026 this run. Nearest verified governance footprint: Uniswap's UNIfication passed Dec 2025 with ~125M UNI in favor vs 742 against (https://www.theblock.co/post/383742/uniswap-passes-unification-proposal)
- **notes**: Cannot be planned into a calendar — include only if a proposal happens to be in its voting window during the demo. Related trap found this run: UNI.totalSupply() still reads EXACTLY 1,000,000,000 (1e27 wei), because UNI has no burn function — UNIfication burns are transfers to a dead address and never move totalSupply. Any UNI-burn market must watch Transfer(to=0x…dEaD), not supply. Burn cadence liveness in 2026 is unverified.
