# Digest: governance

**Summary:** Digest written to /Users/abu/dev/hackathon/buidl-ctc/undefined/technical-creditcoin-usc-attestcoin-2026-08-22/digests/governance-outcomes-r3-1.md (the brief's path variable was literally "undefined", so I placed it alongside the existing technical-topic digests).

THE FEASIBILITY ANSWER, which is the load-bearing finding: almost no Governor emits final vote tallies in a terminal event. I read the contract sources directly. Nouns declares only ProposalQueued(uint256 id, uint256 eta), ProposalExecuted(uint256 id), ProposalCanceled(uint256 id), ProposalVetoed(uint256 id) — id and eta, nothing else. ENS's OZ Governor is the same shape. On both, tallies live ONLY in per-voter VoteCast logs, so reading them means aggregating across N transactions, which the referee forbids outright. Those markets are ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT: a keeper reading proposals(id) / proposalVotes(id) and emitting the numbers in one event.

The exception is Aave Governance V3 (0x9AEE0B04504CeF83A65AC3f0e838D0593BCb2BC7), which emits ProposalQueued(uint256 indexed proposalId, uint128 votesFor, uint128 votesAgainst) and the mirror ProposalFailed with the same tallies. One receipt gives pass/fail AND both exact numbers, and both branches are positive events so there is no absence-proof problem on either side. I decoded a live one: tx 0x4360b937...5659a4 at block 25,683,759, proposal 509, ~383,000 AAVE for vs ~7.2 against. That is the single best governance settlement primitive on mainnet.

CADENCE, measured by direct eth_getLogs over the last ~216,000 blocks (Etherscan was 403/deprecated, so I scanned a public RPC instead — stronger evidence anyway): Nouns 6 ProposalCreated but only 1 ProposalExecuted; Aave 3 ProposalQueued; ENS 1 created / 1 executed; Uniswap GovernorBravo ZERO; Compound GovernorBravo ZERO. Governance is a weekly-to-monthly supply and fails the continuous-supply bar as a product spine. It is flavour, not backbone.

ENDOGENEITY: Nouns is the worst case in the entire slice — a bettor holding ~30+ Nouns can swing a close vote at zero marginal cost because they already own the votes. My recommendation is to never ship a plain "will it pass" market on a whale-decided DAO, and instead sell (a) turnout rather than direction, which is only manipulable by spending real capital and moves the answer honestly; (b) timing ("executed before block B"), which depends on a third party paying gas; (c) the mechanism itself — Nouns' ProposalObjectionPeriodSet fires only when a vote swings late, which no single holder cleanly controls. Plus concentration screening via getPriorVotes at the snapshot block, freezing markets at startBlock, and position caps sized to manipulation cost.

RECURRING SCHEDULED VOTES: yes, Curve gauge weights flip every Thursday 00:00 UTC and voting is continuously contested (71 VoteForGauge in 8.4 days). But there is NO native finalize transaction — checkpoint_gauge emits nothing and weights change by time-based math. You must build the keeper. Balancer's veBAL fork is identical.

Two things I want to flag rather than bury. First, the only demo_fit 5 in this slice is not governance at all: the Nouns daily auction, 27 AuctionSettled in 30 days, price in a single event. And the one settlement I decoded had winner 0x0 and amount 0 — a zero-bid auction — so any price-bucket market needs an explicit "no bids" bucket and the bid distribution sampled before thresholds are chosen. Second, I would not write off Uniswap and Compound on my zero-flow result: Uniswap demonstrably moved this year via UNIfication and references a newer Governor deployment, so those zeroes most likely mean migration, not death. Both are listed as unresolved and worth one follow-up scan.

## Candidates
### 1. Nouns daily auction settlement price (AuctionSettled) — fit 5/5
- **question_example**: What will tonight's Noun sell for — under 1 ETH, 1-5 ETH, above 5 ETH, or no bids at all?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Nouns Auction House proxy 0x830BD73E4184ceF73443C15111a1DF14e495C706 emits AuctionSettled(uint256 indexed nounId, address winner, uint256 amount) — topic0 0xc9f72b276a388619c6d185d146697036241880c36654b1a3ffdad07c24038d99; nounId is topic1, the price is the second 32-byte word of data.
- **cadence**: daily — 27 AuctionSettled events in the 216,000 blocks (~30 days) ending at block 25,813,769
- **uncertainty**: The clearing price depends on who shows up to bid in the final minutes. It is a live English auction with anti-snipe extension; nothing on-chain before settlement determines the number, and it cannot be simulated in advance.
- **manipulation**: A bettor can bid up the auction, but every ETH of manipulation is real ETH actually paid to the Nouns treasury and forfeited if they win. Cost of moving the answer scales 1:1 with the size of the move. No endogeneity problem — bidders are not privileged outcome-makers, they are paying market price.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — the settleCurrentAndCreateNewAuction tx emits AuctionSettled carrying nounId and the final price in one receipt. Example: tx 0xe1f7f1c8e44c758909a7e747664dc3970c8e610ebaeb022f303fb5001707509c at block 25,807,698.
- **live_evidence**: Direct eth_getLogs scan against https://eth.drpc.org: 27 AuctionSettled events at 0x830BD73E4184ceF73443C15111a1DF14e495C706 between blocks 25,597,764 and 25,813,764, most recent at block 25,807,698 (~30h before the scan on 2026-08-22). Decoded log at that block: nounId 0x7ce = 1998, winner 0x0, amount 0.
- **notes**: NOT governance, but it is the only daily Ethereum-mainnet source in this slice and the strongest single-tx settlement found. CAVEAT from the data: the sampled settlement (Noun #1998) had winner = 0x0 and amount = 0, i.e. a zero-bid auction where the Noun is burned. Price-bucket markets MUST include an explicit 'no bids / 0 ETH' bucket, and the bid distribution should be sampled before choosing thresholds.
### 2. Curve weekly gauge-weight flip (Thursday 00:00 UTC) — fit 4/5
- **question_example**: Which Curve pool gets the biggest share of this week's CRV emissions?
- **settlement_mechanism**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT. Curve GaugeController 0x2F50D538606Fa9EDD2B11E2446BEb18C9D5846bB has no finalize event — checkpoint_gauge emits nothing and weights change by time-based math. A small mainnet keeper contract, pokeable by anyone at/after Thursday 00:00 UTC, calls gauge_relative_weight_write(gauge, weekTimestamp) (state-changing, returns the applied weight) and emits GaugeWeightSnapshot(address gauge, uint256 week, uint256 weightBps). That keeper tx is the settlement receipt.
- **cadence**: weekly, forever — weights apply every Thursday 00:00 UTC; underlying VoteForGauge activity measured at ~8.5 events/day
- **uncertainty**: Weights are set by veCRV holders voting continuously right up to the flip, and each voter may change weight only once per 10 days, so the final split emerges from thousands of independent decisions. Not computable in advance.
- **manipulation**: Requires acquiring and locking veCRV (4-year max lock) or renting vlCVX voting power via bribe markets — expensive and slow. Endogeneity is real (gauge voters are often the same people who would bet) but the cost of moving a weight materially is on the order of the bribe market's clearing price, typically five to six figures USD per basis point of emissions. Bound by capping position size relative to that cost.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT — needs the keeper poke tx described above; no native single event carries the applied weight.
- **live_evidence**: Direct eth_getLogs scan against https://eth.drpc.org: 71 VoteForGauge events (topic0 0x45ca9a4c8d0119eb329e580d28fe689e484e1be230da8037ade9547d2d25cc91) at 0x2F50D538606Fa9EDD2B11E2446BEb18C9D5846bB between blocks 25,753,289 and 25,813,769 (~8.4 days), most recent at block 25,811,594. Weekly Thursday 00:00 UTC cadence confirmed by Curve Resources docs.
- **notes**: This is the answer to 'are there recurring scheduled votes with an on-chain finalize tx?' — the schedule exists and is rock solid, but the finalize tx does NOT exist natively; you must build it. Balancer's veBAL GaugeController fork at 0xC128468b7Ce63eA702C1f104D55A2566b13D3ABD is the identical shape. Fixed weekly clock tolerates the ~10-min attestation delay perfectly.
### 3. Aave Governance V3 proposal turnout (ProposalQueued tallies) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: When Aave proposal #N finishes voting, will more than 400,000 AAVE have voted FOR?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Aave Governance V3 core on Ethereum mainnet, 0x9AEE0B04504CeF83A65AC3f0e838D0593BCb2BC7, emits ProposalQueued(uint256 indexed proposalId, uint128 votesFor, uint128 votesAgainst) — topic0 0xe39e7fc9f2013b8ab01110f66610f9fb8675d3126e69b3752f0084afc72be19a. proposalId is topic1; votesFor is data word0, votesAgainst is data word1. The failing branch emits the mirror event ProposalFailed(uint256 indexed, uint128, uint128), so pass AND fail are both positive events.
- **cadence**: ~3 per month — 3 ProposalQueued events in the 216,000 blocks ending at 25,813,769
- **uncertainty**: The DIRECTION is usually a foregone conclusion (the sampled proposal was 383k AAVE for vs 7.2 against), so direction is disqualified. TURNOUT is not: how much AAVE bothers to vote depends on delegate attention that week and cannot be computed or simulated ahead of time.
- **manipulation**: To push turnout past a threshold a bettor must acquire and delegate real AAVE — the sampled proposal moved ~383,000 AAVE, roughly eight figures USD. Endogeneity exists in principle (delegates could bet) but is bounded by cost, and pushing turnout up is the honest direction anyway. Far safer than any Nouns direction market.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — this is the ONLY mainnet Governor found whose terminal event carries the final tallies. Proof: tx 0x4360b93796a5fb0a350d9da4a4aaf73200c566a54e9f3cc84c1b9456165659a4, block 25,683,759, topic1 0x1fd (proposal 509), data 0x5102bb58957ccc21a920 (~383k AAVE for) / 0x6423ba74f7711ed9 (~7.2 AAVE against).
- **live_evidence**: Direct eth_getLogs scan against https://eth.drpc.org: 3 ProposalQueued events at 0x9AEE0B04504CeF83A65AC3f0e838D0593BCb2BC7 in blocks 25,597,765..25,813,765, most recent block 25,683,759. Event signature read verbatim from aave-dao/aave-governance-v3 src/interfaces/IGovernanceCore.sol on main.
- **notes**: Best governance candidate on the referee's rules by a wide margin — but only ~3/month, so it fails the continuous-supply bar as a spine. Note the voting itself happens on the Polygon/Avalanche voting machine and is bridged back via storage proofs; the settlement event nonetheless fires on Ethereum mainnet, which is what matters.
### 4. Nouns last-minute swing (ProposalObjectionPeriodSet) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: Will Nouns proposal #N flip to passing in the final hours and trigger the objection period?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Nouns DAO Governor proxy 0x6f3E6272A167e8AcCb32072d08E0957F9c79223d emits ProposalObjectionPeriodSet(uint256 indexed id, uint256 objectionPeriodEndBlock). It fires ONLY when a proposal swings to a passing state inside the configured last-minute window — a positive event that directly encodes 'this vote swung late'.
- **cadence**: rides proposal flow, ~6 proposals created per month
- **uncertainty**: Depends entirely on the shape of late vote arrival — whether enough FOR votes land inside the last-minute window. No single holder's intent is public in advance and the trigger depends on the ordering and timing of other voters.
- **manipulation**: A whale can deliberately vote late to trigger it, or vote early to prevent it — this is a real endogeneity hole. Bound it by (a) freezing the market at startBlock, (b) refusing proposals where any single delegate's voting power exceeds the expected margin (computable from getPriorVotes at the snapshot block), and (c) position caps. Cheaper to manipulate than any Aave market.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES for the trigger side — one receipt, indexed id, emitting contract and signature all decodable. The 'will NOT trigger' side is an absence proof and needs a deadline settlement tx at the voting endBlock.
- **live_evidence**: Event declared verbatim in nounsDAO/nouns-monorepo packages/nouns-contracts/contracts/governance/NounsDAOInterfaces.sol (master). Governor is live: 6 ProposalCreated events in blocks 25,597,765..25,813,765 via eth_getLogs, most recent block 25,811,309; Nouns app shows proposal #992 created 2026-08-22.
- **notes**: The most charming governance question in the slice — 'will this vote swing at the last minute' is legible in one line. Nouns-specific; no other DAO here has an equivalent event.
### 5. Nouns proposal execution (ProposalExecuted) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: Will Nouns proposal #992 actually get executed?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Nouns DAO Governor proxy 0x6f3E6272A167e8AcCb32072d08E0957F9c79223d emits ProposalExecuted(uint256 id) — topic0 0x712ae1383f79ac853f8d882153778e0260ef8f03b504e2866e0593e04d2b291f. Only the proposal id is carried; no tallies. Counter-outcomes have their own positive events: ProposalCanceled(uint256 id), ProposalVetoed(uint256 id), ProposalQueued(uint256 id, uint256 eta).
- **cadence**: ~6 proposals created per month; only 1 ProposalExecuted in the last 30 days
- **uncertainty**: Outcome depends on future voting by ~750-ish Nouns holders against a dynamic quorum, plus whether anyone pays gas to call execute() after the timelock eta. Not simulatable. Helpfully, ProposalCreated carries startBlock and endBlock, so the market's END TIME is deterministic even though the outcome is not.
- **manipulation**: WORST endogeneity case in the slice. With a small holder set and dynamic quorum near 10%, a bettor holding ~30+ Nouns can swing a close vote at ZERO marginal cost because they already own the votes. Mitigations: sell timing ('executed before block B') rather than direction; screen out proposals where any delegate's power exceeds the expected margin; open the market before startBlock and freeze it at startBlock; hard position caps.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES for the executed side. The NO side is an absence proof — pair it against the other positive terminals (ProposalCanceled / ProposalVetoed) or settle 'executed by eta + grace' with a keeper poke at the deadline.
- **live_evidence**: Direct eth_getLogs scan against https://eth.drpc.org: 6 ProposalCreated (latest block 25,811,309) and 1 ProposalExecuted (block 25,603,992) at 0x6f3E6272A167e8AcCb32072d08E0957F9c79223d in blocks 25,597,765..25,813,765. Tally.xyz shows Nouns proposals 986-992 created 2026-07-23 to 2026-08-22.
- **notes**: Alive and legible, but execution events are thin (1/month) and the direction question is the single most manipulable market in this whole slice. Do not ship a plain 'will it pass' market on Nouns.
### 6. Nouns / ENS final tally or quorum clearance (snapshot keeper) — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: Will Nouns proposal #N clear its quorum — and by how many votes?
- **settlement_mechanism**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT. Neither DAO puts tallies in a terminal event. Build a mainnet keeper contract, pokeable by anyone at/after the proposal's endBlock, that calls NounsDAOLogicV4.proposals(id) (forVotes / againstVotes / abstainVotes) and quorumVotes(id) — or ENSGovernor.proposalVotes(id) at 0x323A76393544d5ecca80cd6ef2A560C6a395b7E3 — and emits TallySnapshot(uint256 indexed proposalId, uint256 forVotes, uint256 againstVotes, uint256 abstainVotes, uint256 quorum). That keeper tx becomes the settlement receipt.
- **cadence**: ~6/month for Nouns, ~1/month for ENS
- **uncertainty**: Turnout is genuinely unforecastable — it depends on how many delegates bother to vote in a given week. Quorum clearance on Nouns is additionally uncertain because quorum is DYNAMIC (it rises with against-votes), so the target moves as votes arrive.
- **manipulation**: Turnout questions are much safer than direction questions: adding voting power costs real capital and moves the answer in the honest direction. Nouns still has the whale problem for close quorum thresholds. ENS delegate concentration is high but the cost of moving turnout is meaningful.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT — confirmed by reading both contracts' event declarations: Nouns emits only ProposalQueued(id, eta) / ProposalExecuted(id); ENS's OZ Governor emits only ProposalQueued(proposalId, etaSeconds) / ProposalExecuted(proposalId). Native tallies exist ONLY as VoteCast(address indexed voter, uint256 proposalId, uint8 support, uint256 votes, string reason) across N transactions, which the referee's rule 3 forbids aggregating.
- **live_evidence**: Nouns event set read verbatim from nounsDAO/nouns-monorepo NounsDAOInterfaces.sol (master). Both governors live per eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org: Nouns 6 ProposalCreated (latest 25,811,309); ENS 1 ProposalCreated (block 25,667,108) and 1 ProposalExecuted (block 25,729,925) in blocks 25,597,768..25,813,768.
- **notes**: This is the precise answer to the feasibility question in the brief: for Nouns and ENS, final tallies CANNOT be read from one event and CANNOT be aggregated. A keeper is mandatory. ENS additionally fails on cadence at ~1 proposal/month.
