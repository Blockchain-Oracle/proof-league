# Digest: defi-rhythms

**Summary:** One candidate clears every referee rule and every quality bar: the Lido daily accounting-oracle report. It fires ~once a day, lands in ONE Ethereum tx, emits TokenRebased from stETH 0xae7ab96520de3a18e5e111b5eaab095312d7fe84 with all five fields needed to compute the day's APR from the receipt alone, and the number depends on ~a third of Ethereum's validator set over the past 24h — un-simulatable and un-manipulable at any sane cost. Liveness is strong and recent: Lido disclosed a specific daily rebase incident on 2026-07-25 (printed 2.04% vs ~2.15% expected; corrected 2026-07-26 to 2.29%) with a post-mortem on 2026-08-06 — that two-day spread is exactly the variance a 3-bucket card wants. ETHDistributed rides in the SAME transaction, giving a free second market on the day's MEV/EL rewards (one proof, two cards). Chainlink ETH/USD is the most legible market on earth but carries two flags: Chainlink is a TRUSTED PUBLISHER on Ethereum (provable that the aggregator emitted the price, not that ETH is worth it), and "the first round after 17:00" is an absence proof — it needs the snapshot-keeper pattern (poke a contract in a bounded window; it reads latestRoundData and emits it). Everything else fails on manipulation or shape: Curve's weekly veCRV distribution ($71.5k in Week 32 of 2026, genuinely varying) is settable by anyone DONATING crvUSD straight to the FeeDistributor for less than a winning bet; Ethena's weekly sUSDe rewards are set by a permissioned rewarder (endogenous) and split across multiple txs; Sky's Smart Burn Engine lets the kicker sandwich the Uniswap trade for near-zero net cost and was cut 87.5% in March 2026; Aave liquidations resolve NO by absence and can be forced YES by self-liquidating a thin position. Curve gauge-weight weekly epochs are disqualified outright — the weights are a deterministic function of votes already cast, i.e. simulatable, the product owner's gas-cost failure mode. Honest gap: all liveness here is docs/forum/news-grade; no Etherscan tx-level evidence was retrievable this run (data.chain.link 429, Curve docs 404/403, Sky developer docs 404), so the Chainlink aggregator address + AnswerUpdated topic0, the Curve CheckpointToken signature, and every Sky mechanism detail remain unverified and must be re-read before wiring a decoder.

## Candidates
### 1. Lido daily rebase (stETH accounting-oracle report) — fit 5/5
- **question_example**: What will stETH's daily yield print at today's Lido report? Under 2.0% / 2.0-2.2% / Over 2.2%
- **settlement_mechanism**: stETH contract 0xae7ab96520de3a18e5e111b5eaab095312d7fe84 emits TokenRebased(uint256 indexed reportTimestamp, uint256 timeElapsed, uint256 preTotalShares, uint256 preTotalEther, uint256 postTotalShares, uint256 postTotalEther, uint256 sharesMintedAsFees) during the AccountingOracle.submitReportData transaction. Answer = daily APR computed entirely from fields inside the one event: ((postTotalEther/postTotalShares)/(preTotalEther/preTotalShares) - 1) * 365d/timeElapsed. Signatures verified this run from lidofinance/lido-dao master Lido.sol; no external data needed by the Creditcoin-side decoder.
- **cadence**: daily, roughly midday UTC — Lido docs say reports arrive 'usually (but not guaranteed) once a day', so the market must be phrased as 'the NEXT TokenRebased after time T' to stay a positive-only settlement immune to a skipped day
- **uncertainty**: The printed number is the realised consensus-layer + execution-layer yield of Lido's entire validator set over the past day: attestation effectiveness, proposer luck, whatever MEV existed in the blocks Lido validators happened to propose, slashings/penalties, and new deposits entering. Forecastable to a band (~2.0-2.3%) but not computable or simulatable in advance. Proven variance: 2026-07-25 printed 2.04% where ~2.15% was expected, and the 2026-07-26 correction printed an extrapolated 2.29% — consecutive days landing in different buckets.
- **manipulation**: Effectively prohibitive. To move the printed APR you must move the rewards of ~a third of all Ethereum validators — not purchasable at any hackathon or even institutional scale. The only real vector is the oracle committee itself misreporting, which is exactly what the July 2026 incident resembled (accidental, disclosed, corrected). ENDOGENEITY FLAG: Lido oracle members could in principle bet on their own report — a footnote for a demo, but for production exclude oracle-member addresses or cap stake.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — one AccountingOracle.submitReportData tx; its receipt carries the TokenRebased log emitted by the stETH address. Status, emitter, topic0 and all seven values are decodable, exactly the Attestcoin receipt shape. Attestation delay (~10 min) is irrelevant for a daily scheduled ending.
- **live_evidence**: Lido Governance forum security disclosure dated 2026-07-25 (update 2026-07-26, post-mortem 2026-08-06) discusses a SPECIFIC daily rebase APR of 2.04% vs 2.15% expected, corrected to 2.29% — https://research.lido.fi/t/security-disclosure-25-7-2026-minor-underreporting-of-total-protocol-cl-side-balances-in-accounting-oracle-report/11756 . Event signatures from https://github.com/lidofinance/lido-dao master Lido.sol; contract address and 'usually once a day' cadence from https://docs.lido.fi/contracts/lido/
- **notes**: THE recommended demo ending. Legible in one sentence, daily forever with zero curation, no keeper needed, no trusted publisher, no absence proof. CAVEAT to re-verify before building: Lido shipped Staking Router v3 in 2026 and the master source now also declares CLBalancesUpdated and InternalShareRateUpdated — confirm on Etherscan that TokenRebased is still emitted by 0xae7ab9... in the most recent report tx, and capture that tx hash as blind-verify evidence.
### 2. Lido daily execution-layer / MEV rewards (ETHDistributed) — fit 4/5
- **question_example**: How much MEV will Lido book today? Under 300 ETH / 300-600 ETH / Over 600 ETH
- **settlement_mechanism**: Same stETH contract 0xae7ab96520de3a18e5e111b5eaab095312d7fe84, same transaction as candidate 1, second log: ETHDistributed(uint256 indexed reportTimestamp, uint256 preCLBalance, uint256 postCLBalance, uint256 withdrawalsWithdrawn, uint256 executionLayerRewardsWithdrawn, uint256 postBufferedEther). Answer field = executionLayerRewardsWithdrawn. Signature verified this run from lidofinance/lido-dao master Lido.sol.
- **cadence**: daily, in the same tx as the TokenRebased report
- **uncertainty**: executionLayerRewardsWithdrawn is the day's MEV plus priority-tip harvest — proposer luck multiplied by whatever MEV opportunity existed that day. Materially MORE volatile day-to-day than the blended APR, because it is not smoothed by the consensus-layer component. Cannot be computed in advance without knowing which blocks Lido validators will propose and what flow lands in them.
- **manipulation**: Prohibitive, same as candidate 1 — you would have to manufacture or withhold MEV across the blocks Lido happens to propose. Same oracle-committee endogeneity footnote applies.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — identical receipt to candidate 1. One proof serves both markets, which is a strong thing to say on stage: one Ethereum transaction, two settled cards.
- **live_evidence**: Same 2026-07-25/2026-08-06 Lido disclosure thread proving the daily report is running — https://research.lido.fi/t/security-disclosure-25-7-2026-minor-underreporting-of-total-protocol-cl-side-balances-in-accounting-oracle-report/11756 ; event signature https://github.com/lidofinance/lido-dao master Lido.sol
- **notes**: Free second market off an ending you are already proving — zero extra proof-pipeline work. Scores 4 rather than 5 only on legibility: 'how much MEV in ETH' takes a beat longer for a newcomer than 'what yield %'. Denominating the buckets in ETH (not USD) keeps it receipt-native.
### 3. Chainlink ETH/USD price round at a scheduled deadline — fit 4/5
- **question_example**: Where does ETH/USD print at Friday 5pm? Under $2,200 / $2,200-2,400 / Over $2,400
- **settlement_mechanism**: TRUSTED-PUBLISHER CANDIDATE — flag explicitly. The ETH/USD aggregator emits AnswerUpdated(int256 indexed current, uint256 indexed roundId, uint256 updatedAt) from the underlying AccessControlledOffchainAggregator (not the proxy). Naive settlement on 'the first round after 17:00 UTC' is an ABSENCE PROOF and violates rule 2. Fix: a snapshot-keeper contract on Ethereum, pokeable by anyone in a bounded window [deadline, deadline+15min], which calls latestRoundData() on the ETH/USD proxy and emits Snapshot(int256 price, uint80 roundId, uint256 ts) — one tx, one emitter you control, one decodable field. Bounty the poke so the window never goes empty.
- **cadence**: several rounds per day — the ETH/USD feed updates on a 0.5% deviation threshold OR a 3600s (1 hour) heartbeat, whichever fires first; more rounds on volatile days. The MARKET cadence is whatever schedule you choose (daily 5pm, or every Friday).
- **uncertainty**: Maximal — it is the price of ETH. Nothing about a future price is computable or simulatable. This is the purest 'depends on future market behaviour' ending available on Ethereum.
- **manipulation**: You cannot move the global ETH price for a hackathon-sized pot; cost is effectively unbounded. The real trust cost is different in kind: Chainlink node operators are a trusted publisher, so the proof honestly demonstrates 'the aggregator contract emitted this number', NOT 'ETH was worth this'. Residual game: a keeper delaying the poke inside the settlement window to pick a nicer price — bounded to noise by a 15-minute window on an hourly-heartbeat feed, and anyone may poke.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT — needs the keeper-poke contract described above. Raw AnswerUpdated logs alone cannot prove a round was the FIRST after a deadline without an absence proof.
- **live_evidence**: PARTIALLY VERIFIED. Feed mechanics (0.5% deviation / 3600s heartbeat, median published with round id and block timestamp) confirmed via https://data.chain.link/feeds/ethereum/mainnet/eth-usd search snapshot and https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds this run; the live feed page itself returned HTTP 429 so NO tx-level activity evidence, aggregator address, or AnswerUpdated topic0 was retrieved. Indirect 2026 liveness: ETH moved +18% on 2026-08-20 with lending protocols pricing off feeds (https://crypto.news/ethereum-aave-defi-liquidation-risk/).
- **notes**: The 'everyone gets it in one second' card — worth keeping for exactly that reason. Scores 4 not 5 because of the trusted-publisher flag plus the snapshot-keeper dependency. MUST re-read the aggregator/proxy address from data.chain.link and topic0 from Etherscan before wiring the decoder; both are currently unverified.
### 4. Curve weekly veCRV fee distribution (crvUSD) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: How much will veCRV holders get paid this Thursday? Under $60k / $60-90k / Over $90k
- **settlement_mechanism**: Curve FeeDistributor — Etherscan labels 0xD16d5eC345Dd86Fb63C6a9C43c517210F1027914 as 'Curve.fi: FeeDistributor' (verify which distributor is the live crvUSD-era one; payouts migrated from 3CRV to crvUSD). checkpoint_token emits CheckpointToken(uint256 time, uint256 tokens), but it may fire multiple times per week (at most once per 24h) so NO single log equals the week's total. Requires a keeper that reads tokens_per_week(weekTs) and emits it in one event.
- **cadence**: weekly, distributed on Thursday
- **uncertainty**: The weekly figure is that week's Curve trading volume plus 80% of accrued crvUSD market interest, swapped to crvUSD. Not computable in advance. Real observed variance in 2026: Week 32 (Aug) $71.5k (+4.6% w/w), Week 16 (Apr) $78k, July avg $105k/wk vs June $124k/wk.
- **manipulation**: CHEAP — this is the disqualifier. The distributor counts its own crvUSD balance, so anyone can DONATE crvUSD directly to the FeeDistributor and inflate the checkpoint. At a ~$70k weekly scale a bettor sizing a position above the donation profits deterministically; any bucket boundary within a few tens of thousands of dollars is simply buyable. Would need bucket widths far wider than plausible donations, or a donation cap.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT — poke a keeper after Thursday's boundary that reads tokens_per_week(weekTs) and emits it in one event. Raw CheckpointToken logs would require aggregating several txs, violating rule 3.
- **live_evidence**: Curve's own newsroom reports a specific, varying weekly veCRV distribution through August 2026: Week 32 2026 = $71.5k, +4.6% w/w — https://news.curve.finance/curve-best-yields/ ; Week 16 2026 = $78k — https://news.curve.finance/curve-best-yields-key-metrics-week-16-2026/ ; July 2026 avg $105k vs June $124k — https://news.curve.finance/curve-monthly-recap-july-2026/ . Contract label from https://etherscan.io/address/0xD16d5eC345Dd86Fb63C6a9C43c517210F1027914
- **notes**: Excellent liveness and genuine week-to-week variance, but the donation attack is direct, cheap, and needs no privileged access — fatal for a judged demo where someone might poke at it. Also only weekly, so it produces one ending per week rather than daily. UNVERIFIED this run: the CheckpointToken signature and the crvUSD-era distributor address (docs.curve.finance/fees/FeeDistributor/ returned 404, resources.curve.finance returned 403).
### 5. Ethena sUSDe weekly staking rewards — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: How much yield will Ethena pay sUSDe stakers this week? Under $8M / $8-15M / Over $15M
- **settlement_mechanism**: StakedUSDe (ERC-4626) at 0x9d39a5de30e57443bff2a8307a4256c8797a3497 receives rewards from the StakingRewardsDistributor and emits a rewards-received event carrying the amount. Per Ethena docs the week's rewards are deliberately paid in MULTIPLE smaller payments across the week, so no single log carries the weekly total — needs a keeper reading totalAssets()/vesting amount and emitting it once.
- **cadence**: weekly — Ethena Foundation computes APY weekly and distributes the following week, split into several payments
- **uncertainty**: Driven by perpetual-futures funding rates, basis spread on delta-hedged positions, and ETH staking yield — pure future market behaviour, not computable in advance. Genuinely uncertain and strongly correlated with things a crypto-native audience enjoys forecasting.
- **manipulation**: ENDOGENOUS — the rewarder is a permissioned Ethena role (REWARDER_ROLE). The entity that decides the number could bet on it, and there is no validator-set-scale cost to setting it differently. Materially worse trust story than Lido, where the number is produced by ~a third of Ethereum's validators that nobody controls. Secondary vector: donating USDe to the vault.
- **single_tx_settlement**: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT — the weekly total spans several payment txs by design (Ethena explicitly splits payments to stop arbitrage), so aggregating logs would violate rule 3. A keeper poke reading vault state is required.
- **live_evidence**: Ethena docs describe the live weekly APY computation and the deliberate multi-payment weekly distribution — https://docs.ethena.fi/solution-overview/protocol-revenue-explanation/susde-rewards-mechanism and https://docs.ethena.fi/protocol-overview/rewards-mechanism . Contract confirmed as an active ERC-4626 token page on Etherscan — https://etherscan.io/token/0x9d39a5de30e57443bff2a8307a4256c8797a3497 . No tx-level date evidence retrieved this run.
- **notes**: Legible and genuinely uncertain, but the permissioned-rewarder endogeneity is the kind of thing a sharp judge asks about, and it is weekly rather than daily. Keep as a second-tier market, not the demo centrepiece. Event signature (RewardsReceived) NOT verified this run.
### 6. Sky (MakerDAO) Smart Burn Engine flap / kick — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: How much SKY gets burned at the next Smart Burn Engine buyback?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Sky's SBE converts protocol surplus into SKY buy-and-burn: vow.flap triggers the Flapper's kick, which routes a fixed USDS lot through a Uniswap SKY/USDS pool and emits an event carrying the lot and the SKY bought. The 'bought' figure is the uncertain value. NOTE: neither the Flapper address, the exact event signature, nor the hop cooldown could be verified this run.
- **cadence**: sub-daily, gated by the hop cooldown once the vow.hump surplus-buffer threshold is exceeded — but the lot size is governance-set and was cut hard in 2026
- **uncertainty**: Weak. The SKY bought for a fixed USDS lot is a function of the Uniswap pool price at kick time — which is observable and simulatable in the seconds before the kick, and the caller chooses the moment. This sits uncomfortably close to the product owner's disqualifying gas-cost example.
- **manipulation**: CHEAP — fatal at hackathon scale. The caller chooses WHEN to kick and the trade routes through a Uniswap SKY/USDS pool, so a bettor can move that pool immediately before kicking and unwind immediately after: the cost of forcing a bucket is only the slippage round-trip, not the size of the bet. With the lot cut to ~37,600 USDS in March 2026, the pool impact needed is small. Additionally the number is increasingly governance-set rather than market-discovered.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES in principle — one flap/kick tx emits one event carrying the bought amount — but the value fails the uncertainty and manipulation bars, so the clean settlement shape does not rescue it.
- **live_evidence**: WEAK / AGGREGATOR-GRADE ONLY. SBE launched Feb 2025 at ~$1M USDS/day; in March 2026 the daily allocation was cut from 300,000 to 37,600 USDS (-87.5%) — https://tokenomics.com/articles/sky-tokenomics-how-the-smart-burn-engine-destroys-102m-in-sky-per-year . An SBE BEAM module lets a trusted operator retune SBE parameters without a core spell — https://forum.skyeco.com/t/smart-burn-engine-bounded-external-access-module-sbe-beam-launch/28149 . Primary docs unreachable: developers.sky.money/core-protocol/smart-burn-engine/ 301s to developers.skyeco.com which returns 404.
- **notes**: Do not build on this for a judged demo. Sandwichable by the very person who triggers settlement, shrinking in economic significance through 2026, increasingly parameter-driven rather than market-driven, and every mechanism detail is unverified because the primary developer docs are 404 after the Sky/skyeco domain migration.
### 7. Aave v3 liquidations (LiquidationCall) — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: Will Aave see a liquidation over $1M today? / Which collateral gets liquidated first after 5pm?
- **settlement_mechanism**: Aave v3 Pool emits LiquidationCall(address indexed collateralAsset, address indexed debtAsset, address indexed user, uint256 debtToCover, uint256 liquidatedCollateralAmount, address liquidator, bool receiveAToken). Signature from prior knowledge, NOT verified this run; Pool address also unverified.
- **cadence**: irregular and bursty — clustered on volatile days, potentially nothing interesting on calm days. Fails the continuous-supply bar.
- **uncertainty**: Genuinely uncertain (market stress), but the uncertainty is attached to a question shape the referee rejects rather than to a clean value.
- **manipulation**: TRIVIAL. Anyone can open a thin position and self-liquidate it to force a YES for a few hundred dollars of gas plus a liquidation bonus they pay to themselves. There is no cost floor at all.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES per individual liquidation, but the natural QUESTION violates rule 2: 'will a liquidation over $X happen today' resolves NO by ABSENCE, which is unprovable. Only the YES branch is provable. Salvageable only as 'the first liquidation after time T — which collateral asset?' with a keeper fallback, and the self-liquidation attack survives that reframing.
- **live_evidence**: Aave v3 is clearly alive and large in Aug 2026: ~$12.2B TVL, $8.98B stablecoins supplied, $7.40B borrowed, 82.46% utilisation; ETH rose ~18% on 2026-08-20 (roughly $1,920 to above $2,270) driving ~$3B of crypto-wide liquidations without cascading Aave — https://crypto.news/ethereum-aave-defi-liquidation-risk/ . A March 2026 stale risk-oracle parameter caused $26-27M in wstETH liquidations — https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/dd516-aave-v3-records-285-million-of-liquidation
- **notes**: The protocol is unambiguously alive; the MARKET SHAPE is what fails. Absence-proof on the NO branch, no guaranteed daily supply of interesting endings, and a self-liquidation attack that costs less than a rounding error. Listed so the parent can rule it out with reasons rather than rediscovering it.
