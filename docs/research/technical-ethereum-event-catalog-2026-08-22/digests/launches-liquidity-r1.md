# Digest: launches-liquidity

**Summary:** This slice is thinner than the brief assumes, and the honest ranking inverts the expected one. Direct eth_getLogs measurements at head block 25,813,756 (not training data) give: Uniswap v4 PoolManager 344 new-pool Initialize events per DAY; Uniswap v3 factory 25/day; Livo Launchpad — the genuine 2026 Ethereum-mainnet pump.fun equivalent — 63 TokenLaunched but only 2 TokenGraduated in 30 days; Sablier Lockup v4 ~11 withdrawals/day. So the one mechanism with a textbook-perfect ending event (Livo's TokenGraduated(address indexed token, uint256 ethCollected, uint256 tokensForGraduation) at 0xaA74Aa89590E3B50BE178eA970E490c173b61110) fires roughly once every 15 days and is forceable for $6-14k by anyone holding the YES side; while the one mechanism with unlimited continuous supply (Uniswap pool creation) is structurally a race, because 'the NEXT pool after time T' cannot be proven first-in-time without an absence proof. Best in slice is 4, not 5. Two findings worth escalating: (1) the precise NFT answer — ERC-721 defines NO sellout event, but _tokenId is INDEXED, so for sequential-ID collections with a fixed known maxSupply N the final mint's Transfer(from=0x0, tokenId=N) DOES prove sellout from one receipt via topics alone; it still fails the continuous-supply bar because every question needs curation. (2) Scheduled vesting unlocks are a confirmed dead end — once the cliff passes, both the claim and the amount are computable from the on-chain schedule, failing the genuine-uncertainty bar outright. The biggest remaining gap: no live 2026 mainnet IDO-style SaleFinalized(totalRaised) mechanism was found, and that event shape would be the ideal fit — worth one more round.

## Candidates
### 1. Uniswap v4 new-pool creation (PoolManager.Initialize) — next-pool attribute race — fit 4/5
- **question_example**: After 12:00 UTC today, which shows up first among brand-new Uniswap v4 pools: one quoting USDC, or one quoting USDT?
- **settlement_mechanism**: PoolManager 0x000000000004444c5dc75cB358380D2e3dE08A90 emits Initialize(bytes32 indexed id, address indexed currency0, address indexed currency1, uint24 fee, int24 tickSpacing, address hooks, uint160 sqrtPriceX96, int24 tick); topic0 = 0xdd466e674ea557f56295e2d0218a125ea4b4f0f6f3307b95f85e6110838d6438. Answer fields: currency0/currency1 in topics[2]/topics[3]; fee, tickSpacing, hooks, sqrtPriceX96, tick all in data. 'Does the next new pool have a hook?' = hooks != address(0), read straight from data word 3.
- **cadence**: daily — measured 344 Initialize logs in the last ~7,200 blocks (~24h), i.e. ~14 new pools per hour
- **uncertainty**: Depends entirely on which anonymous deployers launch pools next hour — the pair, fee tier and hook choice of a not-yet-written transaction cannot be simulated. Genuinely future human behaviour.
- **manipulation**: Creating a v4 pool costs only gas (~$10-30), so a bettor can force any specific attribute to appear. Endogeneity is real: outcome-makers can bet. Mitigated only by race framing where BOTH sides are equally cheap to force, which makes forcing self-defeating rather than profitable. Do not use this for 'will attribute X appear' one-sided markets.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — one Initialize log carries every answer field. CAVEAT: 'the NEXT pool after time T' cannot be proven first-in-time without an absence proof; a submitter could prove any qualifying pool after T, not the earliest. Must be framed as a race resolved by first valid proof submitted, or converted to ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT (keeper extsloads slot0 for a named poolId at the deadline and emits sqrtPriceX96).
- **live_evidence**: Measured this run: 344 Initialize logs in blocks 25,806,556-25,813,756 via eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org; most recent tx 0xd1cf8f8489177953c00ce2f2140ae3885a1b04fedf70bd7f90dbb8959ff8b401 at block 25,813,654 (2026-08-22)
- **notes**: Best in slice purely on volume — a fresh ending every ~4 minutes, forever, with zero curation. Loses a point for the first-ness settlement caveat and for legibility ('will the next new Uniswap pool use a hook' needs a sentence of explanation to a newcomer). v3 PoolCreated is the same shape at 25/day.
### 2. Livo Launchpad bonding-curve graduation (TokenGraduated) — the 2026 mainnet pump.fun — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: Of the tokens launched on Livo today, which one graduates to Uniswap first?
- **settlement_mechanism**: LivoLaunchpad 0xaA74Aa89590E3B50BE178eA970E490c173b61110 emits TokenGraduated(address indexed token, uint256 ethCollected, uint256 tokensForGraduation); topic0 = 0x1669614928af055cd5932e43424383a863eb888f3e3a9f80f443162171c568b6. Answer = indexed token in topics[1]; ethCollected = first data word. Companion event TokenLaunched(address indexed token, uint256 graduationThreshold, uint256 maxExcessOverThreshold), topic0 = 0xcb590522ced23241d2577230dd727d071746ff67d500fa19024dd19959810d4b, publishes the target up front.
- **cadence**: irregular and rare — measured 2 graduations in the last ~216,000 blocks (~30 days); launches are ~2/day (63 in 30 days)
- **uncertainty**: WHICH token graduates and WHEN depends on real buyers filling the curve — not simulatable. But HOW MUCH is nearly deterministic: TokenLaunched pre-publishes graduationThreshold and maxExcessOverThreshold (observed 3.75 ETH and 0.05 ETH), bounding ethCollected to a ~1.3% band. So amount-based questions largely fail quality bar 6; only which/when questions carry genuine uncertainty.
- **manipulation**: Severe. Observed graduation thresholds are only 2-4 ETH (~$6-14k). A single bettor holding YES can buy the bonding curve to completion and force graduation. Outcome-makers can bet, and launching a token on Livo reportedly costs ~$0.30, so an attacker can even manufacture the candidate set.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — one TokenGraduated receipt proves it: contract address, topic0, indexed token, and ethCollected all decodable. Example proof tx 0x7b8393a17c1f7463339535dd4f28373dc0b77bdb6eb520f7deeafd22f32c26cf.
- **live_evidence**: Measured this run via eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org at head block 25,813,756: graduation tx 0xd2dc4382c6343b73673c43b66db86e9f19f2b3580ccc2228f0d6068e8c8966fc (block 25,663,837, 2.05 ETH collected) and 0x7b8393a17c1f7463339535dd4f28373dc0b77bdb6eb520f7deeafd22f32c26cf (block 25,783,679, 3.76 ETH collected); 12 TokenLaunched in the last 7 days, most recent 0x57ff2dee409d0c7def46e9ae1a6a34d63485fc257201a2121ada2eafd0aead0a at block 25,805,018. Addresses from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LivoLaunchpad/livo-contracts/main/deployments.ethereum.mainnet.md
- **notes**: This IS the direct answer to 'what is the 2026 mainnet equivalent of pump.fun and does it emit a graduation event' — yes, and the event is textbook-perfect for the proof system. It fails on cadence (~1 per 15 days) and on manipulation cost. Great as a single scripted demo moment, useless as a continuous market engine.
### 3. Uniswap v3 new-pool creation (UniswapV3Factory.PoolCreated) — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: Which fee tier will the next brand-new Uniswap v3 pool use — 0.05%, 0.3%, or 1%?
- **settlement_mechanism**: UniswapV3Factory 0x1F98431c8aD98523631AE4a59f267346ea31F984 emits PoolCreated(address indexed token0, address indexed token1, uint24 indexed fee, int24 tickSpacing, address pool); topic0 = 0x783cca1c0412dd0d695e784568c96da2e9c22ff989357a2e8b1d9b2b4e6b7118. Answer fields: token0/token1/fee are all INDEXED (topics[1..3]) — fee is directly the answer for tier questions; pool address is in data.
- **cadence**: daily but thin — measured 25 PoolCreated logs in the last ~7,200 blocks (~24h)
- **uncertainty**: Which pair and tier a future deployer picks is future human behaviour, not computable. Note fee tier is heavily skewed toward 0.3% in practice, so tier questions have low entropy — pair-quote questions are better.
- **manipulation**: Same as v4: pool creation costs only gas, so any specific outcome is cheap to force. Endogenous betting is unmitigated in one-sided framings.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — one PoolCreated log settles it, with the answer literally in the indexed topics (cheapest possible decode for the Creditcoin-side verifier). Same first-ness caveat as v4: use a race framing or ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT (keeper calls factory.getPool(tokenX, WETH, fee) at deadline and emits the address; zero = no pool).
- **live_evidence**: Measured this run via eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org: 25 logs in blocks 25,806,556-25,813,756; most recent tx 0x863c14356db33ac7abd9c38157f314a104a6b95dffe1e064e73d49313655fd87 at block 25,813,750 (2026-08-22), token0 0x3b9b70d7f1d6059516d373cfc2959d72f7e922f3 paired with WETH at fee 3000
- **notes**: Strictly worse than v4 in 2026 — 25/day vs 344/day — but its all-indexed event is the easiest thing in this whole slice to decode inside an Attestcoin receipt verifier. Good fallback if v4's tuple-heavy data layout is painful for the decoder.
### 4. Livo TokenLaunched — per-launch attribute markets — fit 3/5
- **question_example**: The next token launched on Livo: will its graduation target be above or below 3 ETH?
- **settlement_mechanism**: LivoLaunchpad 0xaA74Aa89590E3B50BE178eA970E490c173b61110, event TokenLaunched(address indexed token, uint256 graduationThreshold, uint256 maxExcessOverThreshold), topic0 = 0xcb590522ced23241d2577230dd727d071746ff67d500fa19024dd19959810d4b. graduationThreshold is the first data word (observed 0x340aad21b3b70000 = 3.75 ETH), maxExcessOverThreshold the second (0xb1a2bc2ec50000 = 0.05 ETH).
- **cadence**: ~2 per day — 63 TokenLaunched in the last ~30 days, 12 in the last 7 days
- **uncertainty**: The launcher's chosen threshold and tier are unknowable in advance. But the parameter space is small and clustered, so entropy is limited and the market gets stale fast.
- **manipulation**: Very cheap — Livo advertises ~$0.30 to launch a token, so a bettor can simply launch a token with their preferred parameters to settle their own market. This is the worst manipulation profile in the slice.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES per launch, but 'the NEXT launch' has the same first-ness absence-proof problem; only a named-token or race framing is clean.
- **live_evidence**: Measured this run via eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org at head 25,813,756: 63 logs over ~216,000 blocks; most recent 0x57ff2dee409d0c7def46e9ae1a6a34d63485fc257201a2121ada2eafd0aead0a at block 25,805,018 (2026-08-22)
- **notes**: Included mainly because it is the only ~daily cadence Livo offers. Legibility is poor and the $0.30 self-settlement attack makes it unsuitable for real money; usable as a demo filler only.
### 5. NFT collection mint-out (final ERC-721 Transfer) — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: Which sells out first — collection A or collection B?
- **settlement_mechanism**: PRECISE ANSWER: ERC-721 (EIP-721) defines ONLY Transfer, Approval, ApprovalForAll — there is NO sold-out / supply-exhausted event in the standard, so by default the last mint is just another Transfer. HOWEVER _tokenId is INDEXED, so for the common sequential-ID pattern with a fixed known maxSupply N, the final mint emits Transfer(from=0x0 in topics[1], to=minter in topics[2], tokenId=N in topics[3]) — and topics[1]==0 plus topics[3]==N proves 'fully minted' from that single receipt. Fallback for non-sequential/ERC-1155: ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT keeper that calls totalSupply()/maxSupply() at the deadline and emits both.
- **cadence**: irregular and curated — each question requires a human to pick a collection and verify its ID scheme; no self-generating supply
- **uncertainty**: Whether and when a mint sells out depends on real demand — genuinely uncertain and not simulatable.
- **manipulation**: A whale can mint the remaining supply to force a sellout; cost scales with mint price and remaining units, so it is expensive for large collections but trivial for near-complete ones. Outcome-makers (the project team) can obviously bet.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES, CONDITIONALLY — only for sequential-ID ERC-721 with fixed maxSupply fixed at question creation. Breaks for ERC-1155, randomized IDs, and reveal/batch patterns. Otherwise ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT.
- **live_evidence**: unverified — EIP-721 event set confirmed this run at https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721, but mainnet mint-out frequency in 2026 was NOT measured (budget). Treat cadence as unproven.
- **notes**: The indexed-tokenId trick is the genuinely useful finding here and is worth telling the referee about. But it fails the continuous-supply bar outright: every market needs curation, which is exactly what quality bar 7 rules out.
### 6. Sablier Lockup v4 stream withdrawals (WithdrawFromLockupStream) — fit 2/5
- **question_example**: Will stream #4412 be withdrawn from today?
- **settlement_mechanism**: SablierLockup 0x93b37Bd5B6b278373217333Ac30D7E74c85fBDCB emits WithdrawFromLockupStream(uint256 streamId, address to, address asset, uint128 amount); topic0 = 0x40b88e5c41c5a97ffb7b6ef88a0a2d505aa0c634cf8a0275cb236ea7dd87ed4d (resolved via OpenChain signature DB this run). Stream creation emits CreateLockupLinearStream(...), topic0 = 0xbc42cec3f2bd75ce97894dacc83ec6c4b682220d349b5a52d5743e7b46eba2d0.
- **cadence**: ~11 withdrawals/day protocol-wide (76 in the last ~7 days), but only a trickle per individual stream
- **uncertainty**: Whether a given recipient claims today is a private human decision — uncertain. But the AMOUNT withdrawable is fully computable from the vesting schedule, so amount questions fail quality bar 6.
- **manipulation**: Trivially manipulable by the stream recipient, who is a single identifiable party and can straightforwardly bet on their own action. Worst-case endogeneity.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES — one WithdrawFromLockupStream receipt carries streamId, to, asset and amount. But 'will NOT be withdrawn' is an absence proof, so the NO side needs a deadline settlement tx or ONLY-VIA-SNAPSHOT keeper reading withdrawnAmountOf(streamId).
- **live_evidence**: Measured this run via eth_getLogs on https://eth.drpc.org: 216 total logs from SablierLockup over the last ~50,400 blocks (~7 days), of which 76 WithdrawFromLockupStream and 13 CreateLockupLinearStream; most recent activity tx 0xb3f1575b003d8f072e92e01d2a1e670062f88f22e719bc9addb4a3e637acdcc8 at block 25,812,515. Address and v4.0/March-2026 release from https://docs.sablier.com/guides/lockup/deployments
- **notes**: Protocol is genuinely alive on mainnet in 2026, which was worth confirming, but the market shape is bad on every axis except liveness: illegible to a newcomer, thin per-stream supply, single-party manipulation.
### 7. Scheduled vesting cliff unlocks (VestingWallet ERC20Released and similar) — fit 1/5
- **question_example**: Will the team tokens unlock on the 1st?
- **settlement_mechanism**: OpenZeppelin VestingWallet emits ERC20Released(address indexed token, uint256 amount); many bespoke timelocks emit only a plain ERC-20 Transfer from the vesting contract, which carries from/to/amount but no schedule context.
- **cadence**: scheduled calendar dates, typically monthly per project
- **uncertainty**: DISQUALIFYING — once the cliff timestamp passes, both that a claim will occur and how much is releasable are computable in advance directly from the on-chain vesting schedule. This is the 'simulatable' failure mode quality bar 6 explicitly rules out.
- **manipulation**: Not the binding problem; the beneficiary controls timing, but there is nothing uncertain to manipulate.
- **single_tx_settlement**: YES mechanically (one ERC20Released or Transfer receipt), but this does not rescue it — a settleable question with no uncertainty is not a market.
- **live_evidence**: unverified — not measured this run; deprioritised once the computability failure was identified
- **notes**: Listed so the parent does not re-explore it. The brief asked about 'large scheduled unlocks that emit events' — they exist and they emit events, but scheduled means computable, which is fatal. The only uncertain variable is WHEN a beneficiary chooses to claim, which collapses into the Sablier candidate above.

## Not found
- No pump.fun-scale launchpad exists on Ethereum mainnet in 2026. Livo is the closest and only credible analogue found, and it runs at ~2 launches/day and ~1 graduation per 15 days — orders of magnitude below Solana launchpad volume. Any product whose core loop is 'bonding-curve graduation' will starve on mainnet.
- No live 2026 mainnet IDO/ICO launchpad emitting the ideal SaleFinalized(totalRaised)-style event was found. The 2021-era IDO launchpad pattern appears not to run at meaningful cadence on mainnet. Not exhaustively searched — this is the single most valuable gap left for a follow-up round, because that event shape is the perfect fit for the referee's rules.
- Etherscan could not be used for liveness verification — etherscan.io returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches. All liveness evidence was instead gathered by direct eth_getLogs against https://eth.drpc.org and https://ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com, which is a stronger primary source but means no human-readable explorer link is attached to the counts.
- Mainnet NFT mint volume and sellout frequency in 2026 were not measured. The mint-out candidate was scored on structure (it requires per-collection curation, failing the continuous-supply bar) rather than on volume, since curation is the binding constraint regardless of how many mints occur.
- Other 2026 mainnet launchpads besides Livo were not enumerated (budget exhausted). Livo's own marketing claims to be the cheapest ETH launchpad by 10x, implying a small field, but this was not independently verified.
- Whether LivoLaunchpad exposes a public launch counter was not checked. This matters because 'how many tokens will launch on Livo today?' would otherwise need a snapshot keeper, and a keeper contract cannot count past events — it can only read current state.
- Uniswap v4's exact Initialize data-layout ordering was inferred from the canonical signature and confirmed only by successful topic0 match plus plausible field values in live logs; the decoder byte offsets were not verified against the PoolManager source this run.
